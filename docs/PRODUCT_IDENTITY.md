## Mark and language

The selected SVG contains the owner's six aperture paths, original fills,
483 × 507 dimensions and viewBox. Do not change its paths, colors or geometry.
Inspiration screenshots in the archive are not shippable branding assets.

Describe the implemented product as a local-first desktop photo organizer that
keeps originals in ordinary folders. Refer to [build capabilities](CODEX_SPEC.md)
before advertising a feature. Do not describe unimplemented editing, provider
imports or gated backup execution as available.

Use precise actions and evidence language. A preview, similar image, same-disk
copy or completed upload does not establish independent recovery. Never promise
that every possible loss is impossible.

## Persisted identity

Display branding must not rename catalogs, original files, root IDs, catalog UUIDs,
application identifiers, user-data directories, credential scopes or repositories.
Any compatibility migration needs separate review and approval. A catalog contains
references and organization data, not the original media; copying it is not a
photo backup. The filename example is not a claim that arbitrary portable-catalog
opening/import or registered file associations are available.

The owner selected the name. Trademark, domain and app-store clearance is not
established. Naming does not alter the [preservation contract](PRESERVATION_CONTRACT.md)
or [release gates](RELEASE_STATUS.md).
