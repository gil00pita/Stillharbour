# Offline place data

City names, alternate names, centres, population ranks, country labels and region
labels: GeoNames and its contributors, https://www.geonames.org/, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Country and territory names and boundary geometry: Natural Earth,
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/, public domain. Terms:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

Stillharbour derives its local files by selecting fields, joining country/region
labels, sorting records and compressing the result. Country geometry is retained
without simplification. Exact source URLs, acquisition date and SHA-256 hashes
are recorded in manifest.json. No endorsement is implied.

Coverage is limited to the pinned GeoNames cities15000 extract and the bundled
Natural Earth countries/territories. Cities match within 25 km of the listed
centre; this is a nearby area, not a municipal boundary. Country boundaries are
approximate cartographic data, including their source's disputed-area treatment.
Missing cities, outdated names, coastline/border uncertainty and incorrect or
missing photo GPS remain possible. This data is provided without warranty.
