What works:

- The photo stays central in the viewer; tools and editable information occupy distinct areas.
- Catalog-only and original-preservation explanations support trust at consequential decisions.
- Retry, reload and explicit unavailable states provide useful recovery paths.

Priority issues:

1. P1 — Protect unsaved captions and review flags. AssetNotesPanel.tsx:8 keys local state by asset ID, while PhotoViewer.tsx:73 changes photos without consulting dirty state. Navigating away therefore discards an unsaved draft in the source lifecycle. Retain drafts per photo, or offer Save / Discard / Stay before leaving. Keep saves explicit. Verify next/previous, Info close and viewer close with a regression. Suggested command: $impeccable harden.

2. P2 — Keep organization identity and exit visible. metadata.css:1 scrolls the complete dialog, including CatalogOrganizer.tsx:131's title/Close area. Retained catalog-minimum.png and catalog-organization.png show the form scrolled beyond its header. Use a persistent header and independently scrolling body; give Albums & searches and Tag categories separate focused sections. Suggested command: $impeccable layout.

3. P2 — Show saved-search replacement context. CatalogOrganizer.tsx:278–295 replaces a search with the current query while the modal conceals the originating filters. Show saved and proposed filter summaries near the action; provide a reversible review or undo. Suggested command: $impeccable clarify.

4. P2 — Lead with photo tasks, disclose technical rules later. CatalogOrganizer.tsx:337's Unicode/case/slash explanation and its collection/category limits compete with routine actions. InspectionScreen.tsx:304 also exposes “eligible regular files”. Use plain task language, move exact rules/limits into details and retain substantive preservation explanations at the action they govern. Suggested command: $impeccable distill.

5. P2 — Prioritize unlocated photos in Map. MapScreen.tsx:166–189 retains the large map with empty clusters in No usable location mode; :206–211 combines result totals with a different located-photo total. The retained minimum capture corroborates the empty-map emphasis. De-emphasize geography and lead with the unlocated filmstrip and an unambiguous result count. Suggested command: $impeccable layout.

Cognitive load: the viewer is low to moderate; organization is high. Create, select, view, archive, rename, replace filters and arrange categories share a dialog. Hierarchy, one-decision-at-a-time, minimal choices, working-memory support and progressive disclosure need attention. Grouping the sidebar's browsing and protection destinations would also aid scanning without hiding them.

Emotional journey: the quiet photo canvas and preservation guidance establish confidence. Long administrative forms interrupt that calm; losing a caption draft would undermine it. Saving and recovery feedback are strong foundations to preserve.

Persona red flags:

- First-time organizer: “collections”, “memberships” and technical tag rules make creating an album feel unnecessarily complex.
- Frequent photo reviewer: quick next/previous navigation conflicts with unsaved caption work.
- Keyboard/low-vision user: a scrolled-away dialog title/exit increases orientation demands. Escape exists; current focus, zoom and screen-reader behavior remain unverified.
